
# Junior Business Analyst Virtual Internship

A six-week virtual internship completed through YuvaIntern, focused on developing practical Business Analysis skills through simulated business projects and real-world analytical techniques.

## Internship Overview

- **Program:** Junior Business Analyst Internship
- **Platform:** YuvaIntern
- **Duration:** 6 Weeks
- **Start Date:** 14 November 2026
- **End Date:** 26 December 2026
- **Focus:** Business Analysis, Data Analysis and Business Strategy

## Learning Objectives

- Develop practical data analysis and visualization skills.
- Understand stakeholder requirements gathering and documentation.
- Learn business process modelling and optimization.
- Conduct competitor and market analysis.
- Develop data-driven business strategies.
- Communicate business insights through professional reports and presentations.
